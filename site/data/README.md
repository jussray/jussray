# Juss & Co public data contracts

The public site keeps three related truth planes separate:

- `worlds.json` is the founder-declared status, contact, link and evidence source for the six featured homepage worlds.
- `systems.json` preserves technical standalone-system identity and embedded relationships. Standalone does not automatically mean a separate company, price or acquisition funnel.
- `portfolio.json` is the public-safe, non-authorizing projection of the complete canonical Juss & Co portfolio identity map. It may describe role, parentage, technical form and public presentation, but it does not grant merge, deploy, provider, payment or publication authority.

A repository, domain, Lovable/Base44 app, preview, deployment or other carrier is not automatically a distinct public product identity. Internal, duplicate, private-operations-only, legacy and unresolved carriers stay out of the public project index unless their canonical relationship is proven.

Featured does not mean complete. The homepage may emphasize a bounded set of worlds while `/portfolio.html` exposes the broader public-safe company map.

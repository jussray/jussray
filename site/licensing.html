Juss & Co · Data & AI licensing

Original work can create value without giving away the people behind it.

Selected public Juss & Co metadata and original content are available for commercial licensing discussions. Public access is not a blanket license, and private user data is never part of the offer.

Available to discuss

Sanitized public product metadata, founder-authored public case-study material, approved public documentation, and other first-party material that Juss & Co has the right to license.

Never included

Private user or customer records, child or teen data, guardian or family data, private messages, credentials, payment records, private analytics, or internal safety and security material.

Reference is different from training

Search indexing, linking, and short attributed references are allowed. AI training, AI-input ingestion, bulk extraction, dataset reuse, reproduction, and derivative or competing uses require written commercial permission.

Provider-independent by design

The licensing boundary belongs to Juss & Co. A crawler, marketplace, payment provider, or AI vendor can integrate with it later without becoming required for the underlying products to run.

Interested in licensing? Start with the intended use, the material you want, retention expectations, and whether the use includes training, retrieval, evaluation, or redistribution.

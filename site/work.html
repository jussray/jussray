How the work gets done

Small fix. Real path. Clear proof.

01 · UnderstandTranslate the messy goal into the actual human outcome and acceptance criteria.
02 · BuildChoose the smallest reversible implementation that solves the cause, not just the symptom.
03 · ChallengeLook for false confidence, privacy leaks, broken assumptions, regressions and unsupported claims.
04 · VerifyTest the real path and say exactly what the evidence proves, and what it still does not.
